## Protected mathematical distinctions

- `N` is the least realizing order; `n` is the prescribed order.
- Minimal vertex count and radial extremality are separate hypotheses.
- Saturation holds for every invariant polygon within the vertex bound.
- Minimize the nonvertex-image count before area; area is normalized by max|v|=1.
- Side ownership uses `(v_{i−1}, v_i]`; endpoint zero cases are retained.
- A first return is to the selected run of sides, not necessarily its starting vertex.
- No-skipping uses the shorter forward/backward chain and preserves every other return.
- The projective closing defect can require a second derivative.
- The exact real phase is retained alongside the polynomial equation.
- Conjugation reflects all Farey data when the smaller denominator is on the right.
- Negative powers are used for nonzero roots; clearing them can add polynomial zero roots.
- Attainment, branch selection, and maximality are separate steps.
- Farey comparison closes the least-order/prescribed-order gap without circularity.
- Order three has a triangle plus a real segment and a radial discontinuity at π.
- Gauges may be asymmetric. The N⁻³ two-sided rate requires a badly approximable angle.
- Endpoint uniformity is a relative estimate inside open intervals; endpoints have zero deficit.
