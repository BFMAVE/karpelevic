## Mathematical writing and review

- [`proof-drafts/`](./proof-drafts/) — working expository drafts and chapter
  plans. These are not the rendered website source of truth.
- [`proof-audits/`](./proof-audits/) — formal-proof extraction, mathematical,
  source, numerical, accessibility, and implementation audits.
- [`student-reviews/`](./student-reviews/) — two independent linear readings
  of every physical proof page from the perspective of a mathematics student,
  followed by one consolidated recommendation report per page.

The three layers are deliberately separate. A technical audit can establish
that a proof is present and correct without establishing that a student can
understand why each step follows. The student-review reports address that
second question explicitly.
